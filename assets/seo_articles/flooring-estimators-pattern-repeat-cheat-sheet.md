# Flooring Estimator's Pattern Repeat Cheat Sheet

**One page. Print it, laminate it, keep it in the truck.** All rules match Carpet Estimator Pro (free at carpet-estimator-pro.vercel.app). Trim allowance: 3–6 in (US) / 10 cm (UK). Convert sq ft to sq yd: divide by 9.

---

## 1. CUT-LENGTH RULES (straight vs half-drop)

| Pattern | Cut length formula | Example (16 ft room + 6 in trim) |
|---|---|---|
| Plain | room length + trim | 16.5 ft |
| Straight match | round UP (room length + trim) to next full repeat | 2 ft repeat: 16.5 -> 18 ft (9 repeats) |
| Half-drop | round UP (room length + trim + **1.5 x repeat**) to next full repeat | 2 ft repeat: 16.5 + 3 = 19.5 -> 20 ft (10 repeats) |

**Why 1.5x for half-drop:** 0.5 repeat shift at the top of alternating strips + 1.0 full repeat at the bottom to finish the motif. Never cut between repeats — the seam won't match.

---

## 2. STRIPS PER ROLL WIDTH

Strips = room width across the roll, rounded UP.

**US (ft)**

| Room width | 12 ft roll | 15 ft roll |
|---|---|---|
| up to 12 ft | 1 | 1 |
| 12–15 ft | 2 | 1 |
| 15–24 ft | 2 | 2 |
| 24–30 ft | 3 | 2 |
| 30–36 ft | 3 | 3 |
| 36–45 ft | 4 | 3 |
| 45–48 ft | 4 | 4 |

**Metric (m)**

| Room width | 4 m roll | 5 m roll |
|---|---|---|
| up to 4 m | 1 | 1 |
| 4–5 m | 2 | 1 |
| 5–8 m | 2 | 2 |
| 8–10 m | 3 | 2 |
| 10–12 m | 3 | 3 |
| 12–15 m | 4 | 3 |

Widths at exactly the roll width fit on one strip (e.g., a 12 ft room on a 12 ft roll = 1 strip).

## 3. ACCESSORY FORMULAS

| Accessory | Formula | Roll size |
|---|---|---|
| Pad | net area x 1.05 | 270 sq ft |
| Tackless / gripper | perimeter – doorway widths | 4 lin ft battens |
| Seam tape | (strips – 1) x room length x 1.10 | 66 lin ft |

## 5. QUICK REFERENCE — COMMON ROOM SIZES (12 ft roll, 6 in trim)

| Room L x W | Net sq yd | Strips @ 12 ft | Straight cut (2 ft rep) | Half-drop cut (2 ft rep) | Lin ft ordered (straight, +10%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 x 12 | 13.3 | 1 | 12 ft | 14 ft | 13.2 |
| 12 x 14 | 18.7 | 2 | 14 ft | 16 ft | 30.8 |
| 14 x 16 | 24.9 | 2 | 16 ft | 18 ft | 35.2 |
| 16 x 13 | 23.1 | 2 | 18 ft | 20 ft | 39.6 |
| 18 x 20 | 40.0 | 2 | 20 ft | 22 ft | 44.0 |
| 20 x 15 | 33.3 | 2 | 22 ft | 24 ft | 48.4 |

**Lin ft ordered = strips x straight cut x 1.10. Sq yd = lin ft x roll width / 9.**

---

## THE RULE

**Broadloom orders in strips and repeats, not square feet.** Pattern waste is per-cut; width waste is per-strip. A 15 ft roll can beat a pattern penalty — check roll width before you blame the repeat.
